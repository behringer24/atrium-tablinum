# Entry template

Use this structure for new entries. Leave out a section that would stay empty.

```markdown
# <The topic in a few words, as a colleague would search for it>

<Two or three sentences that answer the main question on their own.>

## Details

<The content: rules, numbered steps, facts. One topic only. Short paragraphs, lists where they help.>

## Scope

<Who or what this applies to, from when, and the exceptions. Leave out if it applies to everyone without limits.>

## See also

- <Title of a related entry> (ID <id>)

## Sources

- <Name of the document, author or origin, date; link if there is one>. Filed on <date>.
```

## Guidance

- **Title:** the topic a colleague would search for ("Travel expenses: hotel limits"), not the name of the source ("Minutes of 12 March").
- **Self-contained:** understandable without the source and without the conversation. Explain abbreviations once.
- **Dates are absolute:** "from 1 January 2027", never "next year" or "recently".
- **Binding or not:** say so when the source is a binding rule, and when it is only a recommendation or an experience.
- **Tags:** two to five, lower case. Prefer tags the space already uses (`list_knowledge` shows them).
- **Nothing sensitive:** no personal data of customers, no credentials, no internal details the readers of the space may not see.
